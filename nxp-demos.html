NXP EdgeAI Demos

This catalog contains practical demonstrations for NXP FRDM-MCXN947. Each child project keeps its own setup, status, and limitations as authoritative.

Examples

  • 3D printer spool size ToF uses a TMF8828 8×8 ToF sensor and documents FULL, MEDIUM, LOW, and EMPTY states.
  • EV charger anomaly documents a 20 Hz replay-oriented anomaly demonstration with explicit non-production safety scope.
  • Package transport records anomaly scores, warning/fault transitions, reason codes, and replay evidence.
  • Smart Pong compares human, fixed algorithm, and adaptive EdgeAI control.
  • CGM insulin pump is a research/demo platform, not clinical software.
  • Sphere combines FRDM-MCXN947, LCD-PAR-S035, and FXLS8974CF accelerometer input.
